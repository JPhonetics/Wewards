Wewards, rewarding the relationship that keep businesses growing. Reward valued customers with a free items after accumulating points based on spend or after buying specific items a set number of times. 

Let's reward customers that order the "usus" (usual). Most rewards programs reward points on total spend. Customers are allowed to redeem those points on specific rewards outlined by the company. What if we track the number of times a particular customer orders a specific item. And after a set amount of times we reward them with a free item. For example, a customer has ordered pho on each of their 10 visits. On their next visit, the pho is on us.
